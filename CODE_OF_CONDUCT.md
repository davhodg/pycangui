# Code of conduct

Be respectful to everyone, and keep all contributions relevant to pycangui.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this. Anyone who
repeats this may be blocked, and anyone posting spam, scams or phishing is blocked straight away.

To report something, contact [davhodg](https://github.com/davhodg) privately through their GitHub
profile, or use GitHub's [Report content](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Reports are kept private.
